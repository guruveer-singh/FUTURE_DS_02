🚀 Exciting Project Milestone: Customer Retention & Churn Analysis (Task 2 with Future Interns) 📊

In subscription and recurring revenue businesses, acquiring a new customer is 5x to 7x more expensive than retaining an existing one. Reducing customer churn isn't just a vanity metric—it directly compounds recurring revenue, cash flow stability, and company valuation.

As part of my Data Science & Analytics Internship with Future Interns, I completed an in-depth Customer Retention & Churn Analysis analyzing 7,043 subscriber accounts ($456.1K baseline MRR) to diagnose why customers leave and develop an actionable retention strategy.

🔍 Core Questions Answered:
1️⃣ Why are customers churning, and where is the greatest revenue leak?
2️⃣ Which customer segments and contract types carry the highest attrition risk?
3️⃣ Where in the customer lifecycle does attrition concentrate across tenure brackets?
4️⃣ What high-impact interventions can leadership deploy to maximize retention?

💡 Key Business Findings & Discoveries:
📌 The Contract Duration Multiplier: Month-to-month contracts had an alarming 42.7% churn rate and drove 86.9% ($120.8K/mo) of all lost recurring revenue. Annual commitments reduced churn by up to 15x (down to 11.3% for 1-Year and 2.8% for 2-Year plans).
📌 The "First 12 Months" Onboarding Cliff: Over 55.5% of all churn occurred during the first year of tenure (47.4% first-year churn rate). Attrition stabilized dramatically for accounts active past Month 24, highlighting early onboarding as the primary battlefield.
📌 The Fiber Optic Support Paradox: Premium Fiber Optic subscribers ($91.50/mo avg) churned at 41.9% vs 19.0% for DSL. However, bundling Tech Support and Online Security slashed churn by 54% (down to 22.6%).
📌 Payment Channel Friction: Subscribers paying via manual Electronic Check churned at 45.3% vs 15.2% for automated Credit Card. Auto-pay removes recurring friction and eliminates involuntary churn.
📌 Compounding Customer Spend: Cumulative customer spend expanded by 18.8x from Year 1 ($275) to Year 6 ($5,180), proving that early retention creates massive compounding returns.

🛠️ Tools & Deliverables:
• Microsoft Excel: Designed an executive visual dashboard with KPI cards, native charts, tenure bracket tables, customer risk scoring, and 7,043 cleaned records.
• Python (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn): Engineered automated data preprocessing, cross-sectional tenure curves, and statistical driver analysis.
• Financial Modeling: Built an ROI sensitivity model showing that a 10% churn reduction preserves $166.9K in ARR and adds $1.33M+ in enterprise value.
